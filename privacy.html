Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Lime Landscape Studio (SSM 202603266082 (CA0428872-D), 2, Solok Pegaga, Taman Chi Liung, 41200 Klang, Selangor, Malaysia) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you contact us, including through WhatsApp, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you message us we receive your name as shown on WhatsApp, your phone number, the messages, photos and voice notes you send, and details you share about your property (area, garden size, photos of the space).

How we use it

Only to reply to you, arrange site visits, prepare designs and quotations, carry out the work and follow up afterwards. Some replies may be prepared with the help of software, including AI tools, and are checked against our own rules.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business (for example messaging, hosting and AI providers), and only as needed to serve you, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while we are discussing or doing work for you, and afterwards for warranty, accounting and legal reasons.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop messaging you, or to see, correct or delete your information, at any time by replying "stop" on WhatsApp or writing to us at the address above. We follow Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.
